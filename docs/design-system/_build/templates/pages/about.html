Why governance

Rules only work if they fit how the systems are built

I’ve spent more than 20 years in enterprise IT, starting as a business analyst at Wrigley (Mars), and the last ten years designing data platforms and data products in the cloud. At MediaMarktSaturn I built the customer data platform that 100+ marketers relied on, and at Eviden I architected governed data products and generative AI agents on Google Cloud for enterprise clients. Today I bring that experience to SMEs as an independent AI and data governance consultant.

At Eviden (Atos Group), I ran a Google Agentspace proof of concept and watched AI agents spread through the enterprise much faster than anyone had planned for. Getting an agent working was the easy part. The harder questions were who controls it, what data it touches, and whether it complies with the rules. That’s why I moved into AI governance: to help companies scale AI quickly without losing control of it.
