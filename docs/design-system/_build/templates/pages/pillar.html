Pillar 1 · Governance

Clear owners and rules for every AI system you use

Governance sets how your company decides about AI: who may introduce a tool, who owns it, and which policy applies. It's the base that risk management and compliance build on.

See governance packagesAsk the advisor {{ICON:chev}}

You likely need this if…

  • Teams use AI tools nobody formally approved
  • No one can say who owns which AI system
  • You have no written AI or acceptable-use policy
  • Your board or customers ask "how do you control AI?"

What you get

Concrete deliverables, in your tools

AI inventory

A register of every AI system in use, with purpose, data, vendor and owner.

Responsibility matrix

Who approves, operates and monitors each AI system (RACI).

AI policy set

AI policy, acceptable-use rules and an approval process people actually follow.
