The ivmanto logo

Four approved parts (Nick, 2026-11-10): the tick-v wordmark, the hexagon symbol with the wider upper chevron, the lockup of both, and the short form "iv" + tick for the favicon. All files ready to use are in assets/.

Wordmark

The v is drawn as a teal tick: approved, checked, compliant. Instrument Sans at weight 650 (SIL OFL), outlined, with a custom v. Design: Tally. Use it alone in the site footer, documents, slides and the email signature.

Height is measured from the top of the tick to the baseline. Colours come from the theme: --text for the letters, --brand for the tick.

Symbol and lockup

The symbol is a hexagon cell holding two rising chevrons; the upper, teal one is wider, so the centre reads as moving upward. It goes with the wordmark in the lockup (the website header, 30 px), or alone where the name is already present (a document cover, a slide corner, signage).

Short form, favicon and avatar

For small square spaces the short form "iv" + tick is used (Tally's design): browser tab, app icon, social profile picture. The tick on a teal disc is the advisor's avatar on the site.

Rules

Do

  • Use the files in assets/logo/; never retype the word or redraw the symbol.
  • on-light files on white or light backgrounds, on-dark files on navy or dark ones.
  • mono-black / mono-white only where colour is not possible: fax, embossing, single-colour print.

Don't

  • Don't recolour the tick or the upper chevron in any colour other than the brand teal.
  • Don't stretch, outline, add shadows, or put the logo on photos without a solid panel.
  • Don't use the tick alone as the logo. Alone it is only the advisor's avatar.

Trademark

  • File the wordmark and the tick together as one figurative mark; the symbol is a candidate for a second filing after a clearance search.
  • Font: Instrument Sans, SIL OFL 1.1, with no Reserved Font Name. The logo is outlined artwork, so no font file ships with it. The trademark attorney should confirm this.
  • ™ next to the logo is fine now. ® only after registration.

Files

Every part comes in four colourways: on-light, on-dark, mono-black, mono-white. PNGs have a transparent background; JPGs have a solid one (white for on-light, navy #0a1220 for on-dark).
